Common Questions

Are stove top protectors safe for gas stoves? expand_more
Yes, as long as they are PFOA-free and rated for at least 500°F. Ensure the protector does not cover the ignition source or direct gas output holes.
Can I put them in the dishwasher? expand_more
Most high-quality fiberglass and PTFE liners are top-rack dishwasher safe. However, manual rinsing with warm soapy water often yields longer life.
How often should I replace my protector? expand_more
Under moderate daily use, a premium protector lasts 6-12 months. Replace immediately if you see thinning, tearing, or permanent scorch marks.
