Trustpilot data

Our analysis of the 483 Trustpilot reviews shows a distribution heavily skewed toward 4 and 5-star ratings (roughly 72% total). The remaining 28% is split between 1-star shipping complaints and 3-star durability concerns from users of the "Lite" tier.

Interestingly, users who purchased the "Grip" or "PRO" tiers reported 100% satisfaction with heat resistance, suggesting the higher price point provides measurable utility for high-heat cooking.

BBB and Reddit

The Better Business Bureau (BBB) profile for StoveGuard shows an A- rating, with most complaints centered on customer service response times during peak holiday seasons. On Reddit (r/Cooking and r/HomeMaintenance), the community consensus is that while the protectors are expensive, they "pay for themselves in saved labor" over 2-3 years.

FAQ

Tested up to 500°F, StoveGuard material is designed to resist direct heat but should never touch open flames.

Our take

"If you have a high-end or discontinued stove model, StoveGuard is your only real choice for a seamless look. However, if you are on a budget and have a standard Samsung or LG model, cheaper alternatives may provide 90% of the value for half the price."
